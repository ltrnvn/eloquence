# Resume

## I’m currently working as a *lead frontend engineer* at <ExternalLink link="https://patrowl.io/" text="Patrowl" size="18" />, a cybersecurity platform that helps security teams manage their attack surface.

### Summary
I began my developer journey with an internship as a full-stack developer at <ExternalLink link="https://classicatscattery.com" text="Classicat's"/>. My main focus was to create a new site from scratch with an administration interface and then increase traffic using good SEO practices.

Then I joined <ExternalLink link="https://subskill.com/" text="Subskill"/> — still being a full-stack developer — and for them I have worked on projects involving several fields: luxury fashion brands, banks, public services, health insurance, major retail distributors and e-commerce.

Next, I had the opportunity to work at <ExternalLink link="https://hager.com" text="Hager"/>, a global leader in electrical installations, as a frontend engineer. Our team revamped the main site, meanwhile developing from scratch an SSO widget. It is mainly used by the installers on the ground allowing them to create accounts for customers and navigate through Hager applications without worrying about logins.

In 2023, I joined Patrowl as a frontend engineer, where we rebuilt the whole app on a new stack: from Vue 2, Vuetify and Vuex to Vue 3, TypeScript, PrimeVue, Tailwind and Pinia.

Since 2025, I’ve been leading the frontend team: I define the architecture and best practices, mentor and manage 3 developers, and act as the bridge between Design, Product and Backend.
